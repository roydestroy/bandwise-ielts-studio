**Subject:** You're invited to try Bandwise, AI feedback for IELTS writing and speaking

Hi [name],

Thanks for helping me test **Bandwise**, a workspace for IELTS teachers. You upload a student's essay (typed, photographed or as a PDF) or a speaking recording. Bandwise gives a band estimate for each of the four official criteria, with evidence and next steps. You check and adjust the scores, then print or email a branded report to your student.

**Getting started (5 minutes)**

1. Go to **https://bandwiseapp.com/login** and sign in with Google, or with a code sent to your email.
2. Your account needs my approval first. You'll get an email when it's ready, usually within a day.
3. Start with the **sample student**: click **Try a sample student**. It opens an essay the AI has already marked, so you can see the review and the report without uploading anything.
4. Then add one of your own students and try a real essay or recording.
5. Optional: add your name, logo and colour under **Settings → Branding**, so reports carry your brand.

**Good to know**

- **It's free during the pilot.** AI marking is included: you have **150 credits a month**. A writing assessment uses 1 and a full speaking test 3. You can see what's left under **Settings → AI usage**. If you run out, tell me and I'll raise it.
- **The AI gives estimates, and you have the final say.** Please check and correct the bands before saving. Your corrections are how I measure how close the AI gets to a real teacher, so the more work you review, the better.
- **Student data:** only upload work you're allowed to share. For students under 18, that may mean a parent's consent. Student work is sent to Google Gemini only to produce the assessment, and isn't used to train AI models. Details are in the [privacy policy](https://bandwiseapp.com/privacy).
- **It's an early version.** If something breaks or feels confusing, reply to this email or write to **hello@bandwiseapp.com**. Screenshots help a lot.

**What I'd love from you**

Use it for real marking over the next [4 weeks]. After you've marked a few pieces, Bandwise will ask you **five quick questions** (also under **Settings → Feedback**): what you'd pay for it, and how much time it saves you. That decides the price when it launches. [Optional, only if you mean it: pilot teachers will get a founding discount.]
